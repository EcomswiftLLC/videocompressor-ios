1. What Shrinkly is

Shrinkly is an app for iPhone and iPad that makes video files smaller. You can aim for a target file size or a quality level, use presets for popular apps and services, compress several videos in a batch, compare the original with the result, choose H.264 or HEVC, and save or share the new file. The app can open many video formats, including some that iOS does not play by itself, and saves the result in a format that plays on Apple devices.

6. No promises about results

Compression always trades size against quality. The size you ask for is a target: the real file can differ a little, and a video cannot always be made as small as you ask for without losing too much quality or without a lower resolution. Results depend on the video you start from, on your device and on the format. Some devices cannot encode some formats, and long or very large videos can need a lot of time, storage and battery. Check every result before you delete an original or publish or send a file, and check that it meets the requirements of the place where you want to post it. Third-party services change their file size limits, so a preset is a starting point and not a guarantee.
