```
Make big videos small. Shrinkly shrinks a video to the size you choose, in MB, or by quality, so it fits WhatsApp, an email or a text. It is free, there is no account, and your videos are never uploaded.

SHRINK TO A SIZE
- Set a target in MB with a slider or by typing it, and see a live estimate before you start
- Or choose a quality level and let the size follow
- Shrinkly aims for your target. If a clip cannot reach it without turning to blocks, the app tells you and suggests what to change
- Presets for WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Email (25 MB), Instagram and Reels, TikTok, X, YouTube Shorts and Messages. Services change their limits, so check the preset's target against the app you are sending to

BATCH AND "YOU SAVED"
- Add many videos and compress them in one queue, with progress, time left and a cancel button
- A running counter shows the total space you saved, such as "You saved 3.4 GB"

CHECK THE QUALITY
- Compare the original and the result before you save, with both file sizes side by side

CONTROL THE DETAILS
- H.264 for compatibility, or HEVC for smaller files
- Lower the resolution, reduce 60 frames per second to 30 or 24, change or mute the audio
- Strip location and other metadata, and trim the clip
- Your original is never changed. Shrinkly always saves a new file, and only offers to delete the original after you save

OPENS MORE THAN IOS DOES
- Reads MP4, MOV, M4V and 3GP, and also MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, FLV, MPEG, TS and more, through a built-in open-source engine
- Saves as MP4 or MOV, so the result plays on Apple devices and in the apps you share to
- Videos protected by DRM, such as store purchases and rentals, cannot be processed

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Your videos are not uploaded, there is no cloud queue and there is no account. You choose videos with the iOS photo picker, so Shrinkly sees only the ones you pick, and saving to your library needs add-only access.

FREE, WITH ADS
Shrinkly is free. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it: a banner on the Home screen and, before you save or share, one short video that you choose to watch. A batch needs one ad. If no ad is available, your export goes ahead. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the ads may use your advertising identifier. The app works the same either way.

Try it with the built-in sample clips, no video of your own needed. Shrinkly uses FFmpeg under the LGPL, see Settings > Open-source licences.

Questions or ideas? Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com.
```

## App Review notes (2480 of 4000)

```
Shrinkly makes videos smaller. There is no account, no login and no demo credentials. Everything works on first launch, and no video library is needed.

How to test:
1. Finish the short welcome screens. On Home, tap "Try a sample clip" and choose one of the four clips built into the app (or choose a video from your library, or import a file from Files).
2. On the setup screen, tap a preset (for example WhatsApp or Email 25 MB), or set a size in MB with the slider. The estimate updates live. Open Advanced for resolution, frame rate, H.264 or HEVC, audio and metadata options.
3. Tap Compress. Progress and time left are shown and Cancel works. On the result screen you can see the original and new size, and Compare shows the original and the result side by side.
4. Tap Save (or Share). Batch: add several clips and compress them together; the "You saved" total updates.

Ads: the app is free and supported by Google AdMob. A banner appears on Home only. Before each save, share or export, the app offers one opt-in rewarded video; a batch is one ad. The video is optional to watch, and if no ad is available (offline, no fill, consent pending), the export simply goes ahead without one. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no watermark. A Google UMP consent form may appear first in regions where it is required, followed by the App Tracking Transparency prompt; the app works the same whichever choice is made.

Permissions: Photos (read access through the system photo picker only for the videos the user chooses; add-only access to save results). Tracking (App Tracking Transparency, for ads). The app does not use the camera, the microphone or location.

Open-source software: the app reads formats such as MKV, AVI and WebM with FFmpeg, built LGPL-only (no GPL, no x264/x265) and linked as a dynamic framework. Licences, versions and the source offer are in Settings > Open-source licences and at https://ecomswiftllc.github.io/shrinkly/licenses/ . Build scripts: https://github.com/EcomswiftLLC/ffmpeg-ios-lgpl

DRM-protected video (for example store purchases) cannot be processed; the app shows a clear message and does not try to bypass protection.

Privacy: videos are processed on the device and are never uploaded. The only network traffic is the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Privacy policy: https://ecomswiftllc.github.io/shrinkly/privacy/

Sample clips bundled with the app are from Pexels (Pexels License); credits are in the project documentation.
```
